### Added

- Basetool catalog: the new kinds `mineableElements` and `refiningProcesses`. A mineable element
  carries `resourceTypeGuid` (the `subtypeGuid` of a commodity), `instability` and the other plain
  numbers of the `MineableElement` Record; several elements may share one `ResourceType`, so none
  is picked per commodity. A refining process carries `processName`, `refiningSpeed` and
  `refiningQuality`; the DataForge holds no yields. Additive; `schemaVersion` stays 1 (ADR-0054).
