### Changed

- Basetool catalog: shops get their item list from shop components in every container and from the
  shop prefabs, not only from the shop folders, and the components that later positions of a
  container file hold are found by object name or `CryGUID` (ADR-0073). The placeholder inventories
  `temp_outpost_shopinventory` and `inv_cryastro_dummy` no longer give a shop a list. On LIVE
  310 of 1,184 shops have a list, 53 are unscanned instead of 144. The shape of the catalog does not
  change; `p4kx catalog --json` counts the new cases in `shops`.
