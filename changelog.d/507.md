### Changed

- 3D viewer: the doors, turrets and landing gear of a static model with helper bones now play their
  animations in the viewer. The preview panel of a model keeps its height on every tab and cannot
  be dragged so small that the playback bar covers the view. Play, the position slider and Loop are
  dimmed while nothing can play, and a graphics device that cannot skin a model no longer shows
  playback controls that do nothing.
