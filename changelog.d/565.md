### Changed

- Face animations: long clips are smaller. The sample reduction tolerates 0.5 mm of a joint
  translation, 0.11 degrees of a joint rotation and 0.01 of a morph weight instead of 0.1 mm,
  0.01 degrees and 0.001, so a 151-second idle clip drops from 34.3 MB to 20.7 MB. A head skin
  whose stored control hashes do not match the control names is refused.
