### Changed

- MCP: the containers kept open per Archive have a byte budget (256 MiB; none above 128 MiB is
  kept), a container is decoded outside the cache lock and under the model permit, calls for the
  same container share one decode, and a path inside a container is found through its index.
  `entity_loadout` resolves the Loadout of an Entity once while its pages are read.
