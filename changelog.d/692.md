### Added

- Basetool catalog: the new kind `qualityQuantizations` lists the quantised quality steps of the
  game (`bands` with `start`, `end` and `mappedValue`) and links each to the `ResourceType`s that
  use it by `resourceTypeGuids`. A Record that no `ResourceType` uses is kept and reported as the
  warning `unlinked_quality_quantizations`; a Record that cannot be read is listed as failed. This
  belongs to the unreleased catalog version 2. `read_crafting` returns them as
  `Crafting::quality_quantizations`.
